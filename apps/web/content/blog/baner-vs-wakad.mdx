## How do Baner and Wakad compare at a glance?

Both are established west Pune suburbs popular with IT professionals, but they sit under different civic bodies, at different price points and at different distances from Hinjewadi. The table below summarises what we could verify as of early November 2026.

| | Baner | Wakad |
|---|---|---|
| Civic body | Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) | Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) |
| Position | Between Aundh/Pashan and Balewadi | Between Hinjewadi and Pimple Saudagar |
| Nearest office clusters | Baner-Balewadi business parks; Hinjewadi further west | Hinjewadi's Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, next door |
| Metro Line 3 (from 4 Nov 2026) | Line passes through, but Baner Gaon and Baner stations not yet open | Wakad Chowk station open |
| Average asking rate (Housing.com, 2026) | about ₹10,000-12,100 per sq ft | about ₹8,350-8,750 per sq ft |
| 2BHK rent (ANAROCK, Q1 2026) | No published survey figure found | ₹25,500-33,000 a month |
| Known pain points | Water tankers in dry months, peak-hour traffic on Baner Road | Water tankers, congestion at Wakad Bridge and Bhumkar Chowk |

Housing.com shows two different averages on each locality page, so we give both as a range. These are listing-based asking prices, not transaction prices, and the page does not say whether they are on carpet or built-up area.

## What is the difference between living under PMC and PCMC?

The civic body decides who supplies your water, collects property tax, maintains roads and approves building plans. Baner is in PMC limits; [Wakad](/pune/wakad) is in PCMC. In practice this affects which corporation you deal with for property tax, water connections and complaints, and which development plan governs road widening near your building.

Stamp duty is charged on the higher of the agreement value and the ready reckoner value in both. Work out the exact amount for your property with our [stamp duty calculator](/calculators/stamp-duty-calculator) rather than assuming a rate.

## Which is closer to Hinjewadi, Balewadi and central Pune?

Wakad is closer to Hinjewadi; Baner is closer to Aundh, the university area and central Pune. Balewadi sits between them and is a short hop from either.

- **Hinjewadi:** Wakad adjoins the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, so many residents are a short drive or one metro ride from Phases 1 to 3. From Baner, you cross Balewadi and the highway first.
- **Baner-Balewadi offices:** Several business parks are in Baner and Balewadi themselves, which suits people working there.
- **Central Pune:** Baner Road leads to Aundh and on towards Shivajinagar. From Wakad, the metro is now the more predictable option (see below).

## What is the status of Metro Line 3 in Baner and Wakad?

The Hinjewadi-Shivajinagar line (Metro Line 3) is due to open to passengers on 4 November 2026, after its inauguration on 3 November, with 17 of its 23 stations working. Wakad gets a station in this first phase. Baner's two stations come later.

According to Free Press Journal's 31 October 2026 report, service runs from 6 am to 11:40 pm. The six stations not open in the first phase are Laxmi Nagar, Balewadi Phata, Baner Gaon, Baner (Baner-Pashan Link Road), RBI and Shivajinagar. Trains skip them. No date has been announced for opening these stations, and fares had not been published at the time of the report.

What this means for each area:

1. **Wakad:** Wakad Chowk is in the first phase, as are the Hinjewadi stations to the west and Balewadi Stadium and NICMAR to the east. The line continues to the Civil Court station, where it connects with the existing Maha Metro network.
2. **Baner:** Residents near Balewadi can use the Balewadi Stadium and NICMAR stations, but the stations inside Baner remain closed for now. Until they open, most Baner commuters will still rely on Baner Road.

Once the full corridor is running, an end-to-end trip is expected to take about 45 minutes, compared with about 1.5 hours by road (Free Press Journal, June 2026).

## What is the social infrastructure like?

Both areas are well settled, with schools, hospitals, supermarkets and restaurants within a short drive.

- **Baner** is known for the restaurants and cafes along Baner Road and for Baner Hill, part of the Baner-Pashan Biodiversity Park. Aundh's shops and hospitals are close by.
- **Wakad** has a growing number of schools and colleges and easy access to Pimple Saudagar's shops. The Mhatoba temple and its annual jatra are a local landmark.

Shortlist the specific schools, hospitals and shops you need, and check the drive at the times you would actually travel.

## What kind of housing will you find?

Both areas have a mix of older societies that sell as resale and newer projects that are under construction or recently completed. Mid-to-upper-mid budgets dominate new supply across Pune: ANAROCK says over 51% of the city's new supply in Q3 2026 was in the ₹80 lakh-₹1.5 crore band, and 2BHK and 3BHK flats make up most of what you will see in both areas.

For a resale flat, check the building's age, occupancy certificate, society transfer charges and parking. Our [resale service](/services/resale) covers these checks. For an under-construction flat, check the MahaRERA registration and the promised possession date.

For context, ANAROCK's Q3 2026 report puts Pune's city-wide average base price up about 6% year on year, with available inventory up 5%. This suggests buyers have choice, though it does not guarantee any particular price.

## What known issues should buyers check?

The main issues are water supply in dry months and peak-hour traffic. Both affect Baner and Wakad.

- **Water:** In June 2026, Punekar News reported that societies in Baner and Balewadi were paying about ₹1,200-2,500 per regular tanker, and Wakad societies about ₹1,300-1,500. Monsoon delays and alternate-day PMC supply were the causes. Ask any society you shortlist how many tankers it bought last summer and what they cost.
- **Traffic:** An August 2026 commuter survey under the Unclog Hinjewadi IT Park campaign ranked Bhumkar Chowk third and Wakad Bridge fourth among the most congested points. Baner Road is also busy at peak hours.
- **Construction:** Ongoing metro and road works narrow roads in places. Check the access road to the building, not just the main road.

## Who does each area suit?

**Wakad** suits buyers who:

- work in Hinjewadi and want the shortest commute
- want a metro station in the line's first phase
- want a lower entry price per sq ft than Baner

**Baner** suits buyers who:

- work in Baner, Balewadi, Aundh or central Pune
- value the established restaurant and retail scene
- can pay more per sq ft

For households with one partner in Hinjewadi and one in central Pune, Balewadi, between the two, is worth a look. See our [Baner area guide](/pune/baner) for more.
