## How do Hinjewadi and Kharadi compare at a glance?

Pune's two biggest IT clusters sit on opposite sides of the city: Hinjewadi in the north-west and Kharadi in the north-east. For most people, the right choice is to live near the hub where they work. The table sets out what we could verify as of early November 2026.

| | Hinjewadi (west) | Kharadi (east) |
|---|---|---|
| Governed by | MIDC, PMRDA and gram panchayats; PCMC merger approved in principle, not yet in effect | Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) |
| Main office clusters | Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park, Phases 1-3 | EON IT Park, World Trade Center Pune; Viman Nagar parks nearby |
| Metro | Line 3 open from 4 Nov 2026 (Hinjewadi to Civil Court) | No station yet; Ramwadi-Wagholi extension under construction |
| Airport | Across the city | Roughly 15 minutes by car |
| Average asking rate (Housing.com, 2026) | about ₹8,400-9,250 per sq ft | about ₹9,500-11,500 per sq ft |
| 2BHK rent (ANAROCK, Q1 2026) | ₹24,200-34,500 a month | No published survey figure found |
| Nearby residential pockets | Wakad, Maan, Marunji, Tathawade, Punawale | Wagholi, Viman Nagar, Mundhwa, Keshav Nagar |
| Biggest current issue | Road capacity, waterlogging, civic services | Nagar Road congestion during metro works |

Housing.com shows two different averages on each locality page, so we give both as a range. They are listing-based asking prices, not transaction values.

## Which employers are in each hub?

Hinjewadi is a large, multi-phase industrial and IT estate. Kharadi is more compact, built around a few large business parks.

**Hinjewadi: Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park (about 2,800 acres, developed by MIDC)**

- **Phase 1:** firms such as Accenture, Cognizant, HCLTech, KPMG, LTIMindtree and Tata Technologies
- **Phase 2:** firms such as Infosys, Wipro, IBM, Honeywell, Emerson and Volkswagen IT Services
- **Phase 3:** firms such as TCS, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini, KPIT Technologies and Synechron

**Kharadi and the eastern belt**

- **EON IT Park and World Trade Center Pune** in Kharadi house firms such as Barclays, Zensar, Mphasis, UBS, Citigroup, Allianz and Northern Trust.
- **Business parks in the Yerawada and Viman Nagar area** add employers such as IBM, HCL and Mphasis.

These lists come from public sources and can go out of date. Confirm your own office location before choosing a home.

## What is the commute like in each?

In both hubs, the commute is easiest from the residential pockets right next to the office parks. Both have well-known bottlenecks.

**Hinjewadi.** An August 2026 commuter survey under the Unclog Hinjewadi IT Park campaign named the 10 worst congestion points. Laxmi Chowk topped the list, followed by Wipro Circle, Bhumkar Chowk and Wakad Bridge. Most respondents commuted by private car or two-wheeler, and bus use was negligible. The state has responded. In September 2026, it sanctioned ₹426 crore for internal roads, and about 19 km of roads were proposed for metro last-mile links. The Pune Collector also ordered land acquisition and work on six priority roads to start by 1 December 2026.

**Kharadi.** Punekar News reported in October 2026 that metro construction between Viman Nagar Chowk and Wagholi has narrowed Nagar Road, with the worst congestion on the Viman Nagar-Kharadi-Wagholi stretch. The planned Kharadi-Shivane alternative road was still incomplete. On the plus side, the Keshavnagar-Kharadi flyover, completed in August 2026, removes a roughly 6 km detour for people living on the Mundhwa side.

## What metro and road connectivity does each have?

Hinjewadi is ahead on metro from November 2026. Kharadi relies on roads until its extension is built.

- **Hinjewadi:** Metro Line 3 opened to passengers on 4 November 2026 with 17 of 23 stations. It runs from Maan through the Hinjewadi phases, Wakad Chowk and Balewadi to Civil Court, where it connects with the existing Maha Metro network. Six stations, including two in Baner, open later; the full line was expected by January 2027. The Mumbai-Pune Expressway is also close by.
- **Kharadi:** The nearest metro station is Ramwadi on the Vanaz-Ramwadi line. Preparatory work began in September 2026 on a first phase of about 4.7 km from Ramwadi to Tuljabhavani Chowk, with stations at Vimannagar, Somnathnagar, Kharadi Bypass and Tuljabhavani Chowk. No completion date has been given. By road, the Kharadi bypass links Hadapsar to Nagar Road.

## How easy is it to reach the airport?

Kharadi is much better placed for the airport. Pune airport is in Lohegaon, next to Viman Nagar, and Kharadi is about 15 minutes away by car. From Hinjewadi, the trip crosses the city. A direct metro link to the airport has a finalised alignment, but in March 2026 no stations or timeline had been published. For frequent flyers, this is one of the clearest differences.

## Which residential pockets should you look at?

Most people working in either hub do not live inside the office estate. They live in the suburbs around it.

**Near Hinjewadi**

- [Wakad](/pune/wakad), in PCMC, has established amenities and a first-phase metro station.
- Maan and Marunji are closest to Phase 3 but share Hinjewadi's road and civic gaps.
- Tathawade and Punawale lie north of Hinjewadi near Wakad, also in PCMC.

**Near Kharadi**

- Wagholi, in PMC since 2021, has newer and generally lower-priced housing but sits on the congested Nagar Road.
- Viman Nagar is established, close to the airport, and has retail and colleges.
- Mundhwa and Keshav Nagar connect to Kharadi through the new flyover.

## What known issues should you weigh?

**Hinjewadi**

- **Civic services:** Because Hinjewadi is outside both municipal corporations, water, waste and roads fall to MIDC, PMRDA and gram panchayats. In September 2026, the Collector ordered a faster water supply scheme, new power substations at Lavale and Mahalunge, and a waste plan within a month. None of these had completion dates.
- **Waterlogging:** IT employees reported flooded, potholed internal roads after unseasonal rain in April 2026. They blamed poor drainage and road gradients, and say the problem recurs every year.

**Kharadi**

- **Construction traffic:** Nagar Road congestion is likely to continue while the metro extension is built.
- **Patchy routes:** Alternative road links are only partly complete.

On both sides, ask the society you shortlist about water supply, tanker spending and the access road in the monsoon.

## Who does each suit?

**Hinjewadi and its neighbouring pockets** suit you if:

- you work in the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park
- you want metro access to Balewadi and central Pune from November 2026
- you prefer west Pune's lower portal asking rates

**Kharadi and its neighbouring pockets** suit you if:

- you work in EON, WTC or the Viman Nagar parks
- you fly often
- you want a PMC-governed area

Couples working in different hubs often choose a middle location and accept a longer commute for one partner.
