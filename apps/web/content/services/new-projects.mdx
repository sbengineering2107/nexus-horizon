## What does new project advisory include?

New project advisory covers everything from your first requirement call to booking: shortlisting under-construction projects that fit your budget and location, verifying each one on MahaRERA, comparing pricing and payment plans, arranging site visits, and supporting you through the booking and agreement stage.

## Why buy under construction in Pune?

Under-construction homes usually cost less than ready-to-move flats in the same area and offer staggered payment plans. The trade-off is possession risk, which is why we focus on developers with a consistent record of delivering on time.

## How we work with you

1. **Understand your brief** — budget, preferred areas, configuration and timeline.
2. **Shortlist** — three to five projects that genuinely fit, with honest pros and cons.
3. **Visit** — accompanied site visits on a single day.
4. **Decide and book** — negotiation support, documentation checks and booking.
