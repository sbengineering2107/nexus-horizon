## What does ready-to-move mean?

A ready-to-move home is complete and has an occupancy certificate (OC), so you can move in or rent it out within weeks of registration. You see exactly what you are buying and avoid construction delays.

## Who is ready-to-move right for?

It suits buyers who are paying rent today, families relocating to Pune on a fixed date, and investors who want rental income immediately.

## What we check for you

- Occupancy and completion certificates
- Approved plans against the actual flat
- Society formation and maintenance charges
- Final price, including parking and other charges
