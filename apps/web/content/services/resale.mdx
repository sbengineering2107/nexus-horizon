## How does buying resale work?

Buying resale means purchasing directly from the current owner. You can usually negotiate on price, and the home is ready to occupy, but the paperwork needs more care than a developer sale because ownership has already changed hands at least once.

## Where buyers go wrong

Most problems come from incomplete title chains, unpaid society dues or an existing loan on the property. A structured document check before the token payment avoids them.
